Fascinating Space Facts

Did you know Mars has two moons?
Mars has two small moons, Phobos and Deimos. They are irregularly shaped and may be captured asteroids. Phobos orbits so close to Mars that it completes an orbit in less than 8 hours!
Venus rotates backward
Venus rotates on its axis in the opposite direction compared to most planets in our solar system. This means on Venus, the sun rises in the west and sets in the east!
Saturn could float in water
Saturn is so light that if you placed it in a giant bathtub, it would float! Its density is lower than water, with an average density of about 0.687 grams per cubic centimeter.
